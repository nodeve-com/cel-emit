# Open

What [computed properties](../docs/computed.md) leave undecided.

- A failing field inside a message an expression builds: `emit`'s expression gives one result for the whole literal, so naming `label.text` needs the literal emitted a field at a time.
- Values computed properties share: whether one may fill a property the record's type doesn't print.
- An expression reading something beside the record it fills, such as a second record: a caller's own variable reads only what its Rust can reach without the record.
- A record whose property is another record's type, or its own.
- A nullable computed property: the bridge refuses one, its check taking a value that isn't null.
- The computed example's record schema repeats the answer's schema, since a schema reads no other file.
